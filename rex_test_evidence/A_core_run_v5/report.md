However, they suffer from several critical limitations: thermal runaway during overcharging or short circuits, safety hazards, and limited energy capacity compared to other battery chemistries. ### Advancements in Solid-State Battery Technology
Solid-state batteries represent a significant leap forward by replacing the flammable liquid electrolyte with an inert solid material. This innovation addresses thermal runaway issues and significantly increases energy density, enabling EVs to travel longer distances on a single charge. [Source: [[CLM-01J8Y000000000000000000002]]]
